01 — About us

Where every sound becomes a story

Qbits Records Studios is a creative media house. From the first recorded note to the final published frame, we help artists, businesses, churches and organisations capture their message and share it with the world.

Our work covers videography, photography, video editing, social media management and live streaming, all delivered with creativity, detail and professional presentation.

Record

We capture clean, professional sound and picture so your voice, your event and your story are preserved exactly as they deserve to be.

Produce

Through careful editing, sound enhancement and visual effects, raw material is shaped into polished content that holds attention.

Create

We build campaigns, graphics and online presence that connect brands and individuals with the audiences they want to reach.
